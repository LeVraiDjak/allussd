All USSD privacy policy

Français

Last updated: 23 October 2026

All USSD is a mobile application that gathers USSD/MMI codes and carrier codes country by country, and dials them in one tap. This page describes how your data is handled.

In short: All USSD collects no personal data. The app works entirely offline and without an account.

What the app does not do

  • No user account, no sign-up
  • No internet connection needed to work
  • No personal data collected, transmitted or sold
  • Dialling a code opens your phone's own Phone app with the number filled in: you press Call yourself
  • A WhatsApp line of an operator or a service ("Open WhatsApp" button) opens the chat with that number in WhatsApp, or on WhatsApp's web page if the app is not installed. A first word is sometimes already written, for example "Hi": you send it yourself. All USSD only passes that number and that word to WhatsApp, and sees nothing of the conversation

Data stored on your device

Your settings and your choices in the app (favourites, preferences, codes you add yourself) stay on your phone. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may keep a copy of them in that backup, so you get them back when you reinstall the app or move to a new phone. All USSD has no access to that backup: Android runs it, in your account.

If you ask it to, the app also keeps one phone number, the one you dial most often, so that codes expecting a number can be filled in with a single tap. It is saved only when you press “Save this number”, All USSD never sends it anywhere, and you can see, change or delete it in the app's settings. A secret code is never saved, here or anywhere else: you type it every time.

Sending information, at your initiative (reports, confirmations, suggested codes)

If you use a "Report" button (on a code or on an emergency number), the "This code works" button, or if you suggest a code you added, you choose yourself how to send it: by e-mail, or by opening a form on GitHub. Only what is shown on screen is sent (country, carrier, name and code for a code; country, service and number for an emergency number) and whatever you describe of the problem. Sending is voluntary and triggered by you every single time; the app never transmits anything on its own.

A report opened on GitHub is public and stays public. The other choice is not.

Sharing and reviews

The share buttons (the app, or a code from its sheet) open your phone's own share sheet with a message filled in and the link to the app on Google Play. You choose the app and the recipient: All USSD sends nothing itself and does not know what you shared. For a code, what leaves is its name, its carrier, its country and its code pattern, never the values you typed (number, amount, secret code).

After a few uses, the app may offer to let you review it on Google Play, through Google's in-app review interface. That window is run by Google Play under its own policy: All USSD receives neither your rating nor your text. To pick the moment, it keeps on your device a count of dials and a few dates, without any code. All USSD never sends it anywhere.

Sharing with third parties

We share no data with third parties, since we collect none.

Your rights

All of the app's data lives on your device: you can see it in the app itself and delete it from the app's settings. The copy in Android backup, if there is one, is managed in the backup settings of your phone or your Google account.

Changes to this policy

This policy changes along with the app. The date at the top of the page tells you which version is in force.
