Every USSD code your carrier has, in your pocket. Works offline, no account.

Checking your balance, buying a bundle, finding your own number, sending money: every carrier has its own codes, and nobody remembers them all. All USSD gathers them by country and by carrier, makes them searchable, and dials them in one tap — the app opens your phone's dialler with the code already typed in, and you press Call yourself.

  • 33 countries covered
  • 367 carrier codes
  • 102 emergency numbers
  • 22 interface languages

What sets it apart

  • Fully offline — no connection needed, including when you have run out of both credit and data (precisely the moment you go looking for a code).
  • No access to your data — the app asks for neither your contacts, nor your location, nor permission to place calls on your behalf. Your country and carrier are simply read from the SIM card, with no GPS.
  • Destructive codes locked — among the codes shipped with the app, those that can wipe your phone stay visible but will not dial, with an explanation of what they do.
  • Dual SIM — two carriers selectable at once.
  • Local emergency numbers — police, gendarmerie, fire brigade, ambulance, for every country covered.

Helping out: report a code

Carrier codes change, and no online source is reliable everywhere. Every code in the app is either confirmed on a real handset or marked "unverified" until it is. That is where you can help — two minutes are enough:

A code does not work Suggest a missing code

The forms are in French; your browser will translate them. A report opened on GitHub is public: do not put your phone number or anything personal in it — the country, the carrier and the code are enough.

No GitHub account? Just write to levraidev@gmail.com, saying which country, which carrier, which code, and what happens when you dial it.
